# v15.7 — FPV showcase drone

26 October 2026. This release improves the editable drone starter in response to the owner’s feedback that the previous generic assembly looked too schematic.

## Use it

1. Open **Starter templates** and choose **FPV showcase drone**.
2. Give the copy a project name and choose **Create project**.
3. Use the six assembly stages to reveal hardware, frame, motors, electronics, power and the completed model.
4. Select a part in the canvas or search the assembly list to change dimensions, position, rotation or material.
5. Choose **Present** for an expanded model view with the editing panels hidden. Drag to orbit; use the camera presets. **Back to editing** or Escape restores the editor.

New components are created in a new starter copy. Existing projects are preserved; they are not silently replaced. Existing catalogue components receive the improved geometry and rendering when opened.

## Delivered

- 136 individually editable components, compared with the previous 120.
- Dark carbon frame with chamfered plate corners and a central cutout, procedural weave shading and an open electronics stack.
- Vented motor bells, visible copper windings, machined rings and grounded orange landing bumpers.
- Alternating CW/CCW swept three-blade propellers, with mirrored pitch and corrected outward triangle winding.
- Front FPV camera with lens, housing, pivot screws and an orange open cradle; two rear antenna envelopes.
- Three-wire motor harnesses, a paired power lead, a two-pole connector and a wrapped battery with straps and geometric label markings.
- Carbon / teal / orange finishes, environment reflections, contact shadows and a camera angle that shows the front camera.
- Nine reusable catalogue definitions, bringing the library to 41 parts. Shared frontend/API schema updated.
- A gallery preview rendered from the actual model, plus an isolated presentation mode.
- Material edits now rebuild the part consistently, retaining transforms and wireframe state. The result matches a subsequent reload, rather than temporarily recolouring submeshes that later revert.
- Circular ring generation avoids coincident seam vertices and zero-area triangles. Curved wire tubes have closed ends and complete normal attributes. Bumper thickness is honored exactly; impossible walls are rejected consistently by the frontend and API.

The thumbnail can be regenerated by starting `node scripts/preview.cjs`, then running `node scripts/render-drone-preview.cjs` with the developer’s installed Chrome. The script uses an isolated browser context and never modifies the user’s saved projects. Set `STUDIO_PREVIEW_ORIGIN` when using a different local port.

## Boundaries

This is an editable FPV packaging concept. Component dimensions, blade pitch and cable routes are illustrative. Electrical connectivity, optical performance, motor/propeller compatibility, fastener engagement, structural strength and flight performance are not validated. Decorative surfaces and material appearance are not physical certification. STL and OBJ carry reference geometry; render colours and weave shading are not included in these exports. Composite components are not automatically unioned into single printable solids.

No new Premium gates or prices are introduced. Cloud services and live billing remain unactivated.
